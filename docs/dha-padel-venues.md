# Padel venues in DHA Lahore (research for review)

Researched 2026-10-30 for the DHA map level. Not in the database yet: review this list, then we agree the schema (a `padel` sport, DHA as an area) before seeding.

## Summary

- **33 confirmed** and **12 probable** padel venues inside DHA; 2 more just outside DHA on Bedian Road; 3 rejected.
- By phase: Phase 1: 1, Phase 3: 1, Phase 4: 3, Phase 5: 25, Phase 6: 6, Phase 8: 3, Phase 9 Town: 1, Phase 11: 3, Phase 12: 1, unknown: 1.
- 35 of 45 DHA venues have a location. OpenStreetMap had only 1 of them (Pure Padel).
- Status means what public sources show on 2026-10-30; nobody visited or contacted the venues.

## How this was made

Five independent searches (OpenStreetMap; news and "best padel courts" lists; booking apps and federation pages; venues' own Instagram/Facebook pages; a phase-by-phase sweep) found 292 mentions, merged into 107 distinct venues. Each of the 48 DHA venues was then re-checked by a separate verifier (is it padel, is it open, which phase, where), and a completeness pass looked for missed venues. Only facts were recorded: no reviews, photos or phone numbers.

## Things to decide or check

- **Location sources.** Only one location is from OpenStreetMap itself (Pure Padel); three use the OpenStreetMap outline of the host building or club, not the courts. Locations from the venue's own website, map link or managed booking page are the venue's own facts. Locations marked "directory (Google-derived)" or "Google Maps (via a listing)" come from Google Maps data, which Google's terms do not allow us to store: confirm those (on site, or with the venue) and ideally add the venue to OpenStreetMap before seeding. Booking-site pins are third-party and should be checked too.
- **Phase 5 / Natha Singh Wala edge.** Many Phase 5 venues sit in the Ring Road / Natha Singh Wala strip beside Sector M; sources disagree whether that is inside DHA. OpenStreetMap's Phase 5 outline splits the cluster.
- **Possible duplicates or rebrands** flagged by the searches: Club Padel at Goldcrest Mall became Space Padel; Padel Park and The Padel Club share an address; Pure Padel and Prime Padel Haus are about 80 m apart; iPadel and Js Arena about 17 m apart.
- **School and club courts** (Roots IVY, Future World School, Defence clubs) may be members-only and not open for public pickup games.
