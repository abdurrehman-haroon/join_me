# Research 2: How US universities run house sport, and how campus apps grew (30 Sep 2026)

## House and residential-college competitions
- **Yale Tyng Cup** (since 1933): each game is worth points equal to the number of players on the field (soccer 11, basketball 5). https://intramurals.yale.edu/tyng-cup-point-system
- **Princeton Campus Rec Cup**: points for leagues, fitness events and classes, plus **+50 for the group with the most unique participants**, whatever the results. https://campusrec.princeton.edu/things-do/campus-rec-cup
- **Duke Quad Cup** (2024): points just for attending events, extra for forming teams. https://www.dukechronicle.com/article/2025/01/duke-university-quad-cup-quadex-residential-competition-prizes-second-year-student-affairs-foster-community-school-spirit
- **Harvard Straus Cup**: between the 12 upperclass Houses. First-years compete separately (the Yard trophy). https://www.thecrimson.com/article/2026/5/1/eliot-straus-cup/
- Not verified: MIT and Brown house-cup rules, and women's-only rec hours at these schools.

## Operations layer (IMLeagues / Fusion RecPlay)
- Sportsmanship rated 1–4. Teams averaging under 3.0 miss the playoffs. https://www.imleagues.com/Images/SchoolPhotos/201115135159.pdf
- A forfeit carries a fee. A "default" (notice given before a cutoff) costs no fee.
- Free-agent pools match individual players into teams.
- Stanford runs its own Rec & Well app plus court booking. https://apps.apple.com/us/app/stanford-rec-well/id1555353938

## Consumer apps
- **GotNext / GOATS / ATH** (pickup basketball): pick a court on a map, see who's checked in, tap "on my way". GotNext's pitch: stop texting the group chat. https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gotnext-pickup-basketball/id6782793140
- **Plei** (pickup soccer): 500k+ users across 26 US regions. https://www.plei.com/blog/meet-plei-2-0-building-the-largest-soccer-community-in-the-world
- **Spond**: 1.5M monthly users. Chat, scheduling, attendance and reminders in one free app.
- **GameTime** moved from a free pickup finder to paid club tools (booking, dues). This suggests money comes from organisations, not players.

## Campus-by-campus growth
- **Facebook 2004**: held each school back until its waitlist reached about 20% of students, then opened it. Went from Harvard to 15 schools in under 3 months. https://finance.yahoo.com/news/the-original-facebook-175210454.html
- **Fizz**: a few launch leaders per school, each recruiting about 10 more, plus merch handed out in person. About 50 campuses a month with no paid ads. https://ethicsinsociety.stanford.edu/sites/ethicsinsociety/files/media/file/case_study_fizz1.pdf
- **Yik Yak**: 200 campuses in 7 months, then anonymity made bullying unmanageable and it sold for $1M. Lesson: verified names are a safety feature. https://em360tech.com/tech-articles/why-did-yik-yak-fail-how-messaging-app-died

## Pakistan specifics
- Lahore smog builds from late October and peaks Nov–Dec. AQI passed 1,000 in parts of Lahore in Nov 2024. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_pollution_in_Lahore
- Ramadan: LUMS ran a tape-ball league around iftar in March 2025. https://lums.edu.pk/news/lums-cricket-clash-unites-community
- **SLUMS** (Sports at LUMS) runs 25+ teams and 30+ events a year. It's the natural pilot partner.
- Student numbers (approximate): LUMS ~5,500 · NUST ~14,900 · IBA Karachi ~4,000 · FAST Lahore ~5,500 (sources disagree). GIKI not found.
- Prayer times shift through the year. Show them near kick-off rather than hard-coding them.

## Feature ideas this produced (all now in the roadmap)
1. Points for turning up, not just winning, per hostel (Princeton, Duke)
2. First-year ladder (Harvard)
3. Lightweight fair-play check (IMLeagues sportsmanship)
4. Penalty-free drop-out 2+ hours before kick-off vs. no-show penalty (IMLeagues default)
5. "Who's here now" check-in at a ground (GotNext/GOATS)
6. Free-agent "I'm free to play" pings (IMLeagues)
7. AQI warnings Oct–Jan
8. Prayer times shown near kick-off
9. Ramadan mode
10. Waitlist-gated campus unlock + campus leads (Facebook, Fizz)
11. Society fixtures page (SLUMS)
12. Roster + reminders + chat bundled (Spond)
13. Verified names as a stated safety feature (Yik Yak lesson)
14. Ground booking as the future revenue (GameTime, Stanford)
