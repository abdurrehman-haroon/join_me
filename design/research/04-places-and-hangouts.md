# Research 4: Venue presence and "come join me" hangouts (7 Oct 2026)

## Prior art
- Passive location broadcasting died or was folded into bigger apps:
  - Facebook Nearby Friends, shut 2022: https://www.thurrott.com/cloud/266802/facebook-is-killing-nearby-friends-and-other-location-based-features
  - Zenly, shut 2023: https://intl.life360.com/blog/what-happened-to-zenly
  - Highlight/Banjo (2012), never took off.
- Foursquare survived by selling venue data, not check-ins. Its Swarm spin-off added specials and deals.
- IRL raised $170M and shut when about 95% of its users turned out to be bots. Bought installs are not a network. https://techcrunch.com/2024/07/31/founder-behind-social-media-app-irl-charged-with-fraud
- Timeleft runs weekly stranger dinners and reports about 15,000 guests every Wednesday (its own claim). https://timeleft.com/blog/dinner-with-strangers/
- Bumble rebuilt BFF on Geneva's tech in 2025, moving toward groups and events. https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/18/bumble-bffs-revamped-app-is-here-focusing-on-friend-groups-and-community-building
- Pattern: what lasts is host-led, time-boxed and small-group.

## Safety
- Pakistan: the Digital Rights Foundation logged 3,012 cases of online abuse against women in 2025, mostly on WhatsApp and Facebook. https://digitalrightsfoundation.pk/inside-pakistans-digital-abuse-crisis-new-drf-report-documents-thousands-of-cases-of-online-violence-against-women/
- The main risk is what happens after people meet, so keep chat in-app and ephemeral and never share phone numbers.
- Defaults:
  - Hosts approve strangers.
  - Counts, not identities.
  - Presence is declared, never detected.
  - Public venues only.
  - Women-only hangouts are hosted only by verified women.
- Verification outside campus: phone plus a liveness selfie, then more before someone can host. Show tiers: Campus, Verified, Guest.

## Venues
- CBTL has about 10 branches in Lahore (ProPakistani). Our Places lookup confirmed 3 in DHA: Phase 3, 5 and 6.
- No evidence that Lahore cafés pay for app promotion. They do pay foodpanda commission. Offer venues a free "group coming" signal first.

## Structure
- One app with a place hierarchy (City › Area › Place › Activity). The campus is home and a trust tier.
- Cold start:
  - Show planned hangouts, not an empty "right now".
  - Campus people host at 3–5 nearby venues first.
  - Don't buy installs.

## Sequence
1. Win pickup on campus.
2. Campus people host at nearby venues.
3. Invite verified non-students in DHA.
4. Open Lahore once the report rate and the share of joins that actually meet up hold.
