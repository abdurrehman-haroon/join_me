Recorded activities

What the app would record as you use the phone.

Already decided

  • ＋ starts from what you're looking at: a place card picks that place; a district or the city opens the map there.
  • Location is asked once someone has looked around the place picker: 4 list swipes, or 7 map touches or zooms, or 3 of each, whichever comes first. Not when it opens. The ⌖ button asks straight away.
  • Search covers all of Lahore, ranked by match and then distance, in our own Postgres. No Google.
  • Picking records the result, its position, the typed text, whether location was on, and how it was picked (dot, list or search). Leaving without picking records the last search. The location answer is recorded once.
  • "Start something" stays on place cards.
